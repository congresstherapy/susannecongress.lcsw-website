About Me

Grounded in empathy. Curious by nature.

I am passionate about supporting teens and young adults as they navigate the excitement and challenges of adolescence and emerging adulthood.

I strive to create a welcoming, affirming space where individuals feel seen, heard, and empowered throughout their mental health journeys. I work with people of all ages and backgrounds, helping them understand how their life experiences shape their well-being.

I have a special interest in treating young adults with anxiety and trauma-related disorders. In addition, I have extensive experience providing gender-affirming mental health care and supporting individuals who identify as LGBTQIA+.

My approach is grounded in empathy, curiosity, and collaboration, with a focus on equipping clients with practical skills and greater self-awareness to navigate life with confidence.

Approach

Evidence-based, tailored to you

I draw from several modalities, adapting my approach to fit each person's needs.

Cognitive

CBT

Identifying and reshaping the thought patterns that fuel anxiety and distress.

Skills-based

DBT-Informed

Practical tools for emotion regulation, distress tolerance, and interpersonal effectiveness.

Exposure-based

ERP

Exposure and response prevention for anxiety, OCD, and avoidance patterns.

Values-driven

ACT

Building psychological flexibility and living in line with what matters to you.

Present-focused

Mindfulness

Grounding practices to build self-awareness and steady the nervous system.

Depth-oriented

Relational Therapy

Understanding how past experiences and relationships shape present-day patterns.

Insurance

Berkeley SHIP accepted

I am in the UC Berkeley SHIP provider network. I am not on any other insurance panels. Check with your insurer if you are uncertain whether my services will be covered. I can provide you with a monthly statement to submit to your insurance, HSA, or FSA plans.

Fees

Session rate

My current rate is $220 for a 50–60-minute hour. I reserve several spaces for individuals who cannot afford my full rate.
